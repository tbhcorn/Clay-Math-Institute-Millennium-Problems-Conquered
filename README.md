# Clay Math Institute Millennium Problems Conquered

**Author:** Daniel Carlo Crivelli Grilli  
**Date:** May 2026  

## Abstract & Philosophy
Known by the majority of people as dreadful, mathematics, applied on a macro scale, helps us define how the world operates. At a microscopic level, it shows us how the world functions. Physics combines math and logic to show us how the world is an infinite resource with neverending opportunity. 

This paper demonstrates how an over-complication of mathematics has led the collective to believe that the "hardest" mathematics mysteries can only be solved through immense complexity. Instead, this manuscript asserts that these mysteries can actually be solved via basic logic, discrete structures, and binary principles (io). Logic is the universal language.

## Millennium Problems Addressed
This repository contains the formal manuscript addressing the seven Millennium Prize Problems through the lens of logic and linear algebra:
1. P vs NP
2. Hodge Conjecture
3. Navier-Stokes Equation
4. The Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Conjecture
5. Yang-Mills Existence and Mass Gap
6. Riemann Hypothesis
7. The Poincaré Conjecture
